How to play

Seven prompts. Each asks for one thing from a wide category — “Name a fish”, “A capital city in the Americas”. Read every qualifier.

25 seconds. Type one answer and press Dive. A rejected answer doesn't end the round — try again while time remains. Time out or pass and the prompt scores zero.

Rarer is deeper. An accepted answer lands in one of six tiers. Every point is 10 metres. A perfect dive is 700 points — 7,000 m.

Unlimited. Prompts are generated fresh every dive.

How rarity is measured

Claude checks that your answer is valid and gives its canonical name. Rarity then comes from real corpus frequency data (the open wordfreq dataset — 316,000 English words drawn from Wikipedia, film subtitles, news, books, Reddit and Twitter).

For each prompt the game builds a pool of the 20 answers most people would give, looks up how often each appears per million words, and compares your answer against the typical (median) pool answer: 4× as common or more is Plankton; under 0.04× is One in a Krillion.

If a word is ambiguous in writing (“bass”, “Washington”), Claude's own read of the rarity is used instead. Both numbers are shown under every result.
